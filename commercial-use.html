Commercial use of node-opcua

node-opcua uses an open-core model. The core SDK is MIT-licensed — you can use it in commercial products with no obligation to Sterfive. Commercial add-on modules and professional services are available for teams that need more.

Open core MIT

The full OPC UA client and server SDK, including subscriptions, alarms & conditions, historical access, security, and more. Free to use, modify, and distribute.

  • Client & server SDK
  • Subscriptions & monitored items
  • Alarms & conditions
  • Historical access
  • Security (PKI, certificates, encryption)
  • XML nodeset import/export
  • 3,000+ tests, ~97% coverage
